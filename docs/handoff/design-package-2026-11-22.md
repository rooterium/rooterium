# Rooterium — developer handoff package

Nov 22, 2026 · the editor

## What Rooterium is

Rooterium is a reference website about Armenian culture for the Armenian diaspora — people who want to reconnect with their roots. It works like Wikipedia but with no user edits: all content is prepared by the team, so no moderation is needed.

This document has everything needed to build it: the concept, the user journey, the visual style, mood boards, approved images and video with links and prompts, and a recipe for generating new assets in the same style.

## Concept: a forgotten temple deep in the soul

The whole site is an abandoned temple carved into a cliff, like the Armenian monasteries of Geghard or Khor Virap. It stands for the depth of the soul, where a people's forgotten memories are kept. A visitor from the diaspora enters and walks deeper, toward their roots.

- **Everything is a memory.** Images are faded, blurred and scratched, like old photos found in a box.
- **Faces are hidden** behind a pomegranate, a hand, a book or a veil: we don't remember them clearly. The mystery is in what is not shown.
- **The point-and-shoot flash** works like a flashlight in the dark cave: it lights up a memory for a moment, then it fades back into darkness.
- **Roots** (hence the name): the tree of life with its roots is the recurring symbol; its six roots match the six sections.
- **Only Armenian.** Costumes, architecture and symbols must be recognisably Armenian: the taraz (folk costume), the veghar (the pointed black hood of Armenian monks), khachkars (carved cross-stones), the arevakhach (eternity sign), pink tuff stone. Generic “magical” imagery (a wizard in a pointed hat) does not fit.

## User journey

The home page is the entrance to the temple, and each section is a separate hall deep inside it. The visitor doesn't switch pages — they walk through the temple.

## Visual style

The style is a forgotten memory shot on old film: a point-and-shoot snapshot with direct flash, or a frame from a 1960s art-house film. The main reference is the film *The Color of Pomegranates* (1969).

**What makes a frame “ours”**

- **Depth.** A blurred foreground (a pomegranate branch, a candle, coins, lace), a sharp object or figure in the middle, a blurred background with an overexposed doorway or a shaft of light.
- **Imperfection.** Scratches, dust, light leaks, uneven exposure, heavy grain, slight blur.
- **Faded colour.** A grey, muted palette shifted toward green and rose, deep shadows. One accent colour: pomegranate red.
- **Living paintings (tableaux vivants).** Static frontal compositions, symbolic objects: pomegranates bleeding juice onto white linen, manuscripts, a dagger, silver coins, candles.
- **Double exposure** for symbols, religion and the manuscript: two times or two images show through each other.

**Avoid** (tested and rejected): brightness, a fairy-tale look, golden glow, gloss, sterile cleanliness, flat frames where everything is in focus, non-Armenian costumes.

**For the site UI** (a suggestion, not yet approved): dark background, text in old-paper colour, an old serif typeface, light grain and film flicker over the whole site.

## Section: Symbols

The Symbols section is a dark chamber deep below the chapel. The visitor descends a stone staircase and is met by a single ancient khachkar (carved cross-stone) standing alone in the darkness. Below it, the symbol categories open up for reading.

**Flow**

1. **Descent.** In the chapel the visitor picks “Symbols”. The descent video plays: the camera turns away from the woman, finds a staircase in the rock and walks down into the dark.
2. **The khachkar.** The video ends on the hall image (below). Over it, fade in the section title and one line, e.g. “Symbols are the language of memory.”
3. **Categories.** Scrolling down reveals category cards, each with its own cover in the same style.
4. **Symbol pages.** Inside a category: one card per symbol — image, history, meaning, where it appears.

## How to generate new assets

Images: Higgsfield, **Soul Cinema** model, 2k quality, about 0.12 credits per image. Video: **Kling 3.0** with a first and a last frame. Nano Banana and Cinema Studio were tested: their output is too clean and flat.

**Prompt template** — pick one opening, describe three planes, add texture and mood:

```text
[OPENING — pick one of three]
A) Real 35mm film frame from a forgotten 1969 Armenian poetic art film, expired Soviet color stock, worn vintage lens.
B) A forgotten old photograph found in a box, shot on expired film with a cheap point-and-shoot camera and harsh direct flash.
C) A faded double exposure on expired film, like a forgotten memory from an old family album.

[FOREGROUND] Foreground heavily out of focus, very close to the lens: <pomegranate branch / candle / strings of silver coins / hand with a silver ring>.
[MIDGROUND, IN FOCUS] <main object or figure; if a person, the face is hidden behind an object; costume is Armenian: taraz / veghar>.
[BACKGROUND] <soot-black walls of a cave temple with khachkars / shaft of light from the dome / overexposed doorway>, fading into darkness.
[TEXTURE] Film scratches, dust, light leak, halation, uneven exposure, faded grey, green and rose tones, crushed blacks, heavy grain, vignette.
[MOOD] Esoteric, sacred, silent, haunting, like a half-remembered dream.
```

**Rules:**

- Spell out the architecture: “Armenian church, pinkish tuff stone, conical dome, no onion domes”, otherwise you get a Russian or Byzantine church.
- Don't generate text in images: captions and the ROOTERIUM name are added in the layout.
- One colour accent per image: pomegranate red.
- For video transitions, make both frames in Soul Cinema first, then give them to Kling as start / end and describe the motion as one slow camera move.
